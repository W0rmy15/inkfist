Hands

How to hit

  • A D or arrows walk. W jumps — tap for a short hop, hold for a full one. S crouches.
  • Shift dashes. Hold back to backdash. In the air, dash toward the stick; up+Shift chases a launch.
  • J light. J, J, J is a string. Forward+J is a kick.
  • K launcher uppercut. Down+K is a sweep. Forward+K carries them into the wall.
  • L energy blast. Crouch under it. Forward+L up close is a throw. Down+L, or K+L, spends a full meter on the super.
  • I blocks. Tap it as the hit connects to parry.
  • In the air, J and K juggle. Down+K spikes — they bounce off the ground.
  • After the launcher connects, press W to jump-cancel and follow.

Gamepad: stick or d-pad to move, A light, B heavy, X special, Y or LB block, RB dash.
